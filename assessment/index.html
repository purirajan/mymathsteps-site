The free 10-minute math check-in.

A student can take this alone or with a parent or tutor—no sign-in is needed. They answer 10 questions, one from each major topic for the grade, and see a short explanation after every answer.

Choose a grade

It’s early in the school year, so choose the grade your child just finished. For example, a 7th grader would choose Grade 6. This year’s topics may not have been taught yet.

Grade 8? Take the full test, which covers Grades 6, 7, and 8.

How to use the check-in
  • About 10 minutes, on a phone, tablet, or computer. There’s no timer.
  • The student can work independently or with a parent or tutor. Pencil and paper help; a calculator isn’t needed.
  • After each answer they see a short explanation, so it feels like practice, not an exam.
  • At the end review the results together and decide what to explore next.

One question per topic shows where to look closer. It isn’t an official test. Answers stay in this browser tab. Tutors can use and review the check-in without an account; a parent account is only needed if a family wants to save and track results.
