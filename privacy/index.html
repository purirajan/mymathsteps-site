Privacy Policy

The short version

  • Accounts are for parents and guardians only. Children never create accounts, and we never ask for a child’s name, email, photo, or location.
  • We store very little about students: a nickname and grade chosen by the parent, and topic-level scores from saved tests. Individual answers are not stored.
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling data. We don’t use cookies, analytics services, or advertising tools, and we never sell or rent personal information. We only keep simple daily counts, like how many check-ins were started, that can’t identify anyone.
  • You’re in control. You can review, download, or permanently delete your data at any time from your parent dashboard.

1. Who we are

MyMathSteps (“we”, “us”) operates mymathsteps.com, a math practice and progress-reporting website for Grades 6–8 students, their families, and tutors who support them. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. Contact us at privacy@mymathsteps.com.

2. Information we collect

From parents and guardians

  • Account information: your email address and password. Passwords are stored in hashed (scrambled) form by our authentication provider; we can’t see them.
  • Your confirmation that you are an adult parent or guardian, and your consent when you add a student profile.
  • Account activity: when the account was created and when you last signed in, used for security and data retention.

About students (provided by the parent)

  • Student profile: a nickname you choose (we ask you not to use a full name) and a grade level.
  • Saved results: for each test or check-in you save, the date, grade, test version, total score, and the number of questions correct in each topic area (for topic tests, also at each difficulty level).

If you sign up for launch updates

  • Your email address, if you enter it in the “Get notified” form. We use it only to tell you when new features launch, such as Grade 8 check-ins and practice lessons. This form is for parents and guardians.

What we do not collect

  • Children’s names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, voice, or location.
  • Individual answers to test questions. Answers are kept only in the open browser tab while the test is in progress, and only the topic-level totals are saved.
  • Payment information. MyMathSteps is currently free.

Technical information

We do not use cookies, analytics services, advertising tools, or tracking pixels. To understand whether the check-in is useful, we keep anonymous daily totals of how many check-ins, full tests, and topic tests are started and finished, and how many check-in results are saved. These totals are just numbers per day; they contain no account details, IP addresses, or device information, and can’t be linked to you. Your sign-in session and any test in progress are kept in your browser tab’s temporary storage and are cleared when the tab is closed. Like all websites, our hosting and database providers may automatically record technical data such as IP addresses and browser type in security logs. We do not use these logs to identify or profile users.

The short check-in at /assessment/ can be used without an account. Its answers stay in the browser and are not sent to us unless a signed-in parent chooses to save the result to a student profile.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the service: run tests, save results, and show your progress dashboard.
  • To send account emails, such as email confirmation and password resets.
  • If you signed up for launch updates, to email you about new features. You can unsubscribe at any time by replying to any update or emailing us.
  • To show anonymous comparisons (see section 5).
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

We do not use your information for advertising, we do not build marketing profiles, and we do not use student information to train artificial intelligence models.

4. Children’s privacy

Students may take the short check-in without an account, including with a tutor before a session. To save results to a student profile, an adult parent or guardian manages the family account, chooses the student’s nickname and grade, and gives consent. Children do not create accounts or provide personal information to us. This approach is designed to respect the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

As a parent or guardian you can, at any time:

  • Review the information stored about your student in the parent dashboard.
  • Download all of your data using “Download my data” in the dashboard.
  • Delete a student profile and its results, or delete your entire account.
  • Stop further collection by not saving results or by removing the student profile.

If you believe a child has created an account or given us personal information, contact privacy@mymathsteps.com and we will delete it.

5. Anonymous comparisons

To show how a student’s full-test result compares with others, we combine results from all students who took the same grade and test version into anonymous totals: the number of students, average and median scores, how many students fall into each 10-point score range, and average topic scores. Each student counts once, using their latest attempt. Comparisons appear only after at least 10 students have taken the same test. Other parents never see your student’s nickname, individual score, or any other information about your family.

6. Who we share information with

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. We use a small number of service providers that process data on our behalf, under their own security and privacy commitments:

  • Supabase: database and sign-in (stores account and student information).
  • Resend: sends account emails and launch updates (receives your email address).
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website files (may log technical data such as IP addresses).

We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety of our users or the service. If MyMathSteps is ever sold or merged, we will notify you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

7. How long we keep information

  • We keep your account and student data while your account is active.
  • Accounts with no sign-in for 24 months are deleted automatically, together with all student profiles and results.
  • Sign-ups that never confirm their email address are deleted after 7 days.
  • Launch-update emails are kept until you unsubscribe, or until we stop sending launch updates, whichever comes first.
  • When you delete a profile or your account, the data is removed from our database immediately. Copies may remain in our providers’ encrypted backups for a short period (up to 30 days) before being overwritten.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Database security rules ensure each parent can access only their own family’s information, and anonymous comparison totals are calculated on the server so individual results are never sent to other families. Account access requires a verified email address and password. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a security incident affecting your information, we will notify you as required by law.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, including California and other U.S. states with privacy laws, you may have rights to know, access, correct, download, and delete your personal information, and to not be discriminated against for using these rights. You can do most of this yourself in the parent dashboard, or email privacy@mymathsteps.com. We will respond within the time required by law. We do not sell or share personal information for targeted advertising.

10. Where the service is offered

MyMathSteps is intended for families in the United States. If you use it from another country, your information will be processed in the United States.

11. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and notify account holders by email before the changes take effect. We will ask for your consent again before using student information in a materially different way.

Questions? Email privacy@mymathsteps.com.
