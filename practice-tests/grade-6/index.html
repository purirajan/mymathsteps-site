    2. Rewrite each fraction: 3/4 = 9/12 and 1/6 = 2/12
    3. Add the numerators: 9/12 + 2/12 = 11/12
    4. Can 11/12 be simplified? No

    3. Check by estimating: each bar costs a bit less than $1, so 10 bars cost a bit less than $10

    3. Check: 6 × 9 = 54
  7. Shapes and angles

    Two angles are supplementary. One of them is 128°. What is the other angle?

    1. Supplementary angles add up to 180°
    2. Subtract: 180° − 128° = 52°
    3. Check: 128° + 52° = 180°
  8. Area and volume

    A box is 8 cm long, 5 cm wide, and 3 cm tall. What is its volume?

    1. Area of the base: 8 × 5 = 40 square cm
    2. Multiply by the height: 40 × 3 = 120
    3. Volume is measured in cubic cm, so the volume is 120 cubic cm
  9. Data and averages

    Five quiz scores are 82, 90, 75, 88, and 95. What is the mean?

    1. Add the scores: 82 + 90 + 75 + 88 + 95 = 430
    2. Count the scores: 5
    3. Divide: 430 ÷ 5 = 86
    4. Check: the mean is between the smallest score, 75, and the largest, 95
  10. Chance

    A bag has 3 red, 5 blue, and 2 green marbles. You pick one without looking. What is the probability that it is blue?

    1. Total number of marbles: 10
    2. Number of blue marbles: 5
    3. Probability = blue ÷ total = 5/10 = 1/2
