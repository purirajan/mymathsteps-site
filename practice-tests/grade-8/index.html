  1. Square roots

    Estimate √75 to the nearest tenth without a calculator.

    1. Perfect squares on each side of 75: 64 and 81
    2. So √75 is between 8 and 9
    3. 75 is closer to 81, so √75 is closer to 9
    4. Test: 8.6 × 8.6 = 73.96 and 8.7 × 8.7 = 75.69
    5. 75 is closer to 75.69, so √75 ≈ 8.7
  2. Powers and big numbers

    Multiply (3 × 10⁴)(4 × 10⁵). Write the answer in scientific notation.

    1. Multiply the numbers: 3 × 4 = 12
    2. To multiply 10⁴ × 10⁵, add the exponents: 4 + 5 = 9
    3. So the product is 12 × 10⁹. The first number must be less than 10: 12 = 1.2 × 10
    4. Answer: 1.2 × 10 to the power 10
  3. Equations

    Solve 4(x − 2) = 2x + 6.

    1. Distribute the 4: 4x − 8 = 2x + 6
    2. Subtract 2x from both sides: 2x − 8 = 6
    3. Add 8 to both sides: 2x = 14
    4. Divide by 2: x = 7
    5. Check: 4(7 − 2) = 20 and 2(7) + 6 = 20
  4. Slope and straight lines

    A line passes through (−1, 4) and (3, −4). Write its equation in the form y = mx + b.

    1. Change in y (rise): −4 − 4 = −8
    2. Change in x (run): 3 − (−1) = 4
    3. Slope m = rise ÷ run = −2
    4. Use the point (−1, 4) to find b: 4 = −2(−1) + b, so b = 2
    5. Equation: y = −2x + 2. Check with (3, −4): −2(3) + 2 = −4
  5. Functions

    For f(x) = 3x − 5, find f(−2). Then find x when f(x) = 16.

    1. Substitute −2: f(−2) = 3 × (−2) − 5 = −6 − 5 = −11
    2. Set up the equation: 3x − 5 = 16
    3. Add 5 to both sides: 3x = 21
    4. Divide by 3: x = 7
  6. Two equations together

    Solve the system y = 2x + 1 and y = −x + 10.

    1. Both equal y, so set them equal: 2x + 1 = −x + 10
    2. Add x to both sides: 3x + 1 = 10
    3. Subtract 1, then divide by 3: x = 3
    4. Substitute into y = 2x + 1: y = 7
    5. Check in the other equation: −3 + 10 = 7. The solution is (3, 7)
  7. Moving and resizing shapes

    Triangle ABC has corners A(1, 2), B(4, 2), and C(1, 5). Dilate it by a scale factor of 2 from the origin.

    1. To dilate from the origin, multiply each coordinate by 2
    2. A′ = (2, 4)
    3. B′ = (8, 4)
    4. C′ = (2, 10)
    5. Side AB is 3 units long, so side A′B′ is 6 units long
  8. Right triangles

    A 13-foot ladder leans against a wall. Its foot is 5 feet from the wall. How high up the wall does it reach?

    1. The ladder is the hypotenuse, c = 13. One leg is a = 5
    2. Use a² + b² = c²: 25 + b² = 169
    3. Subtract: b² = 144
    4. Take the square root: b = 12 feet
  9. Volume

    A cone has a radius of 3 cm and a height of 8 cm. What is its volume? Use 3.14 for π.

    1. Volume of a cone = ⅓ × π × r × r × h
    2. Square the radius: 3 × 3 = 9
    3. Multiply by the height: 9 × 8 = 72
    4. Take one third: 72 ÷ 3 = 24, so the volume is 24π cubic cm
    5. Using 3.14: 24 × 3.14 = 75.36 cubic cm
  10. Scatter plots and tables

    A line of best fit for test score (y) and hours studied (x) is y = 5x + 60.

    1. The slope is 5: each extra hour of study goes with about 5 more points
    2. The y-intercept is 60: the predicted score with 0 hours of study
    3. Predicted score for 4 hours: 5(4) + 60 = 80
    4. As hours go up, scores go up, so the association is positive
