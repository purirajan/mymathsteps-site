  1. Negative numbers and fractions

    What is −8 ÷ 2 + 3 × (−4)?

    1. Divide first: −8 ÷ 2 = −4
    2. Then multiply: 3 × (−4) = −12
    3. Add the results: −4 + (−12) = −16
  2. Ratios and proportions

    On a map, 2 cm stands for 15 km. Two towns are 7 cm apart on the map. How far apart are they really?

    1. How many km does 1 cm stand for? 15 ÷ 2 = 7.5 km
    2. Multiply by the map distance: 7 × 7.5 = 52.5 km
    3. Check with a proportion: 2/15 = 7/x, so 2x = 105 and x = 52.5
  3. Percents and money

    Shoes cost $64. They are 25% off, and then 8% sales tax is added. What is the total?

    1. Discount: 25% of $64 = $16
    2. Sale price: $64 − $16 = $48
    3. Tax: 8% of $48 = $3.84
    4. Total: $48 + $3.84 = $51.84
  4. Expressions

    Simplify 4(2x − 3) − 5x + 7.

    1. Distribute the 4: 8x − 12 − 5x + 7
    2. Combine the x-terms: 8x − 5x = 3x
    3. Combine the numbers: −12 + 7 = −5
    4. Simplified: 3x − 5. Check with x = 1: both forms equal −2
  5. Equations

    Solve 3x − 7 = 20.

    1. Undo “− 7” by adding 7 to both sides: 3x = 27
    2. Undo “× 3” by dividing both sides by 3: x = 9
    3. Check: 3 × 9 − 7 = 20
  6. Steady rates

    5 pounds of apples cost $13.75. The cost is proportional to the weight. How much do 8 pounds cost?

    1. Constant of proportionality (cost for 1 pound): $13.75 ÷ 5 = $2.75
    2. Equation: y = 2.75x, where x is pounds and y is dollars
    3. Cost of 8 pounds: 2.75 × 8 = $22.00
  7. Shapes and angles

    Two lines cross. One angle is 64°, and the angle opposite it is (2x + 10)°. Find x, and the angle next to the 64° angle.

    1. Vertical (opposite) angles are equal, so 2x + 10 = 64
    2. Subtract 10 from both sides: 2x = 54
    3. Divide by 2: x = 27
    4. The angle next to 64° is on a straight line with it: 180° − 64° = 116°
  8. Area and volume

    A circle has a radius of 5 cm. What is its area? Use 3.14 for π.

    1. Area of a circle = π × r × r
    2. Square the radius: 5 × 5 = 25
    3. Area = 25π square cm
    4. Using 3.14: 25 × 3.14 = 78.5 square cm
  9. Data and averages

    Find the median and the mean of 4, 9, 6, 12, and 9.

    1. Put the numbers in order: 4, 6, 9, 9, 12
    2. The median is the middle number: 9
    3. The sum is 40, so the mean is 40 ÷ 5 = 8
  10. Chance

    You flip a coin and spin a spinner numbered 1 to 4. What is the probability of heads and an even number?

    1. Number of possible results: 2 × 4 = 8
    2. Results that are heads and even: heads and 2, heads and 4, so 2 results
    3. Probability = 2/8 = 1/4
  11. Word problems

    Four movie tickets cost $9.50 each, and popcorn costs $12. You have $60. How much money is left?

    1. Cost of the tickets: 4 × $9.50 = $38
    2. Add the popcorn: $38 + $12 = $50
    3. Money left: $60 − $50 = $10
    4. Check by estimating: about 4 × $10 + $12 = $52, so about $8 left. $10 is reasonable.
